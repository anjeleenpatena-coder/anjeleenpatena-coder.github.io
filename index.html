Buying from abroad?

Many of my clients are OFWs. You can do almost everything online.

Can I buy a property while I'm abroad?

Yes. We can do viewings by video call and send documents online. Payments can be made from abroad.

Do I need an SPA?

If a family member will sign for you here, you'll need a Special Power of Attorney. I'll guide you on how to prepare it.

Can I apply for a Pag-IBIG or bank loan?

Yes. OFWs can apply for home loans. I'll help you check the requirements and prepare your papers.

Can I see the property online?

Yes. I can do a live video tour so you and your family can see it together.
